AiKOVA · UK · Eurozone · Africa

Europe’s AI Receptionist. Starting with the UK.

AiKOVA is an AI receptionist for restaurants, cafés, clinics, salons, retail and professional services. It answers phone calls and WhatsApp, books tables and appointments, handles FAQs, and can send payment links (Stripe in the UK/EU, Paystack / M-Pesa in Africa). Most businesses go live in about 10 minutes.

What AiKOVA does
  • Answers inbound voice and messaging 24/7 in multiple languages
  • Books reservations and appointments into your calendar
  • Sends deposit / payment links and logs conversations in a Gift Inbox
  • GDPR-ready for UK & Europe · NDPR-ready for Africa

Languages

English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Yorùbá, Naija Pidgin, Hausa, Igbo, Kiswahili, isiZulu and Afrikaans — with region adapters for Twilio (UK/EU) and Africa’s Talking (Africa).

Who it is for

High Street restaurants and cafés, clinics and pharmacies, salons and barbers, retail, and professional services that miss calls during rush hours. AiKOVA adapts to your menu, hours and services from your website.
